Who I am

A little more

I’m studying for a B.S. in Civil Engineering at Louisiana State University, on track to graduate in May 2029. What draws me to it is the part you can walk around — structures, loads, and the physical logic of how a thing holds together.

Outside of class I work in LSU’s School of Leadership & Human Resource Development, where I support faculty research and help run an NSF-funded program for STEM teachers. I’ve also spent three years as a freelance writer, helping university applicants shape essays that still sound like them — work that taught me how much of any technical field is just explaining it clearly.
