Approach

Three things I keep coming back to

Different surfaces, same instinct: take the thing that is hard to see, and make it plain.

01

Structures you can see

Forces and loads are real but unseeable. Learning to draw them properly — in three dimensions, where you can move around them — is where the understanding actually starts.

02

Tools that carry the dull load

The first thing I ever built was a scheduling system that kept a haulage CEO from double-booking himself. Build the small unglamorous thing well, and the attention it frees goes to the work that needs judgment.

03

Saying it clearly

Three years of helping applicants find the sentence they actually meant. Engineering is not so different — a design nobody can follow is a design that does not get built.
